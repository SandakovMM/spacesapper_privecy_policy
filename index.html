Space Sapper Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This policy explains how the Space Sapper game ("the game") handles your information. In short: the game does not collect, store or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not connect to any server of ours.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your settings (such as sound and music volume) and your game progress locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the game.

The free demo version keeps a count of how many games have been started, using Android's Block Store service, so the demo limit still applies after a reinstall. This count is only a number. It is not linked to your identity, and Google may include it in your device backup according to Google's own privacy policy. We cannot access it.

Sharing with third parties

We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.

Children's privacy

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
